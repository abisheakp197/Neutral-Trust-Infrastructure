## What is UBE?

UBE is a sovereign backend automation platform built for organizations that cannot afford to trust third-party infrastructure with their data, identity, or operations.

It replaces dozens of fragmented services — auth, events, queues, encryption, compliance, identity, automation — with a single unified platform that you fully own and control.

**Your data never leaves your infrastructure. Your operations never depend on our uptime.**

- **Enterprises** that need full data sovereignty with zero vendor lock-in
- **Regulated industries** (healthcare, finance, government, legal) with strict compliance requirements

- **Organizations** that have been burned by third-party outages, breaches, or pricing changes

## What It Does

UBE gives you a complete backend platform in a single deployable package:

**Data & Events**
- Real-time event streaming and processing
- Exactly-once message delivery
- Distributed transaction coordination

**Security & Identity**
- Cryptographic identity management
- Multi-factor authentication
- Role-based and attribute-based access control
- Post-quantum encryption

**Compliance**
- GDPR, HIPAA, CCPA, DPDP, LGPD — built-in, not bolted on
- Automated compliance reporting
- Data subject request handling

**Automation**
- Intelligent workflow orchestration
- Self-healing infrastructure
- Predictive auto-scaling
- Autonomous anomaly detection and remediation

**Integration**
- Connect to any third-party service
- Cross-organization identity federation
- Protocol translation (SAML, OIDC, and more)
